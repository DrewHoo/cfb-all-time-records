This is a list of seasons completed by the Mississippi State Bulldogs football team . Representing Mississippi State University , the Bulldogs compete in the Southeastern Conference West Division in the NCAA Division I FBS . Since 1914, Mississippi State has played its home games out of 61,337-seat Davis Wade Stadium in Starkville, Mississippi . Initially known as the Mississippi A&M Aggies, the program began play in 1895 as an independent before joining the Southern Intercollegiate Athletic Association the following season. The school did not field a football program from 1897 to 1900. In 1921, Mississippi A&M joined the Southern Conference , where they competed for twelve seasons before joining the SEC where they currently reside. The Bulldogs won their first and only SEC championship in 1941 , and captured a division title in 1998 . As of the end of the 2017 season, Mississippi State has played in 24 bowl games, compiling a 14–10 record. They have played in a bowl game in eleven consecutive seasons dating back to 2010 . The team is currently led by head coach Jeff Lebby.
